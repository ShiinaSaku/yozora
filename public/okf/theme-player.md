# Creditless Theme Player

Yozora integrates directly with the [AnimeThemes.moe](https://animethemes.moe) open community archive to deliver clean, creditless anime openings (OP) and endings (ED).

## Features
- **1080p Video Sequences**: Clean video sequences without text credits or broadcaster watermarks.
- **Audio Deck**: Persistent floating audio player with playback speed and visualizer.
- **Artist Metadata**: Full track titles, artist credits, and season tags.
- **Rich Schema**: Linked with Schema.org `MusicPlaylist` and `MusicRecording` definitions.
